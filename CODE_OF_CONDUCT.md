# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want Friday's community to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
Contributions and conversations are welcome in **English and Spanish**.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind, patient and constructive; assume good intent.
* Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
* Focus on what is best for the users and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
* Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
* Spam, including unsolicited promotion and mass-generated issues or pull requests.
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@Ale-Newport](https://github.com/Ale-Newport)) may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour is harmful. Report problems privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Ale-Newport/Friday/security/advisories/new) (choose a
title starting with "Conduct:") or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[community impact guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
